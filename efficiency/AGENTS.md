# Efficiency Pass

Efficiency Pass holds intervention intent, break-even reasoning, and qualification contracts for the immediately preceding Harness pass. It does not optimize the original product work, independently evaluate itself, or own ordinary implementation.

## Read first

- Read `DOCTRINE.md` completely for evidence, recursion, break-even, and retirement invariants.
- Read `PLAYBOOK.md` before improving one completed Harness pass.
- Use the installed target's compact pass telemetry or current-state command before opening broad trajectory, memory, or source context.
- Read the Harness map only when placement, discoverability, or qualification remains unresolved.

## Boundary

- Efficiency Pass owns one observed Harness-pass tax, one break-even hypothesis, its expected future encounter point, and the later `retain`, `revise`, or `remove` question.
- Harness owners own prompts, maps, selective context, tools, adapters, validation, delivery, cleanup, qualification, and retirement paths.
- The independent evaluation owner owns comparison validity and realized-efficiency conclusions.

## Rules

- Review the exact preceding Harness pass, not the original ordinary job.
- Select at most one coherent earliest-owner change and prefer a measured no-op when encounter or break-even is not credible.
- Do not stack recursive interventions, count delivery as savings, or trade ordinary outcome quality, authority, proof, portability, or recovery for lower trajectory cost.
- Keep outcome, cached and uncached processing, tool work, elapsed and waiting time, retries, human attention, risk, and carrying cost separate when evidence permits.

## Current decision status

Resolve the preceding Harness intervention or no-op, delivered identity, placement, expected encounter, telemetry coverage, observed tax, active Efficiency intervention, break-even, and promised comparable exposure. Record intervention `available`, `retrieved`, `invoked`, and `relevant` separately when later evidence exists.

## Validate and exit

Run the scaffold validator and the target's normal checks. Deliver through the owning workflow, verify artifact identity and cleanup, state active surfaces added and retired, and leave the decision `pending` until later comparable exposed Harness work supports `retain`, `revise`, or `remove`.
